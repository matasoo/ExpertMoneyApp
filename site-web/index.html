Security

Your finances,
nobody else's business

Financial data is the most sensitive thing on your phone. We treat it that way.

Face ID / Touch ID lock

The app locks itself the moment you switch away. Nobody opens it but you.

Privacy Mode

One tap blurs every balance on screen — for the bus, the office, or a nosy friend.

Isolated by account

Server rules make your records readable only by you. Not by other users. Not by us.

Delete everything

Erase your account and every record it holds from inside the app, whenever you want.
